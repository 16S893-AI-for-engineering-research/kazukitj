01 / Introduction

Hi, I’m Kazuki.

I’m a third-year graduate student pursuing a PhD in AeroAstro. I’m in the Gas Turbine Lab, where I study the thermofluids of aerospace propulsion.

02 / Research

Studying flow in aerospace propulsion.

For the past two years, I worked with Professor Ed Greitzer on compressor rotating stall.

This fall, I started a new project with Professor Masha Folk to automate the extraction of flow-mechanism information from CFD solutions using AI.

03 / Why 16.S893

Using LLMs well for research.

LLMs have improved massively and rapidly in the past few years, with noticeable progress every few months.

I think that, when set up well, an LLM could add the kind of productivity that comes from working with another graduate student. I want to learn how to use these tools well to make the most of my research and maximize the discovery of scientific knowledge in my field.
