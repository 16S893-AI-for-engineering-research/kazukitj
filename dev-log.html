6 October 2026

Developing testing ideas for the course project.

Whilst I have not implemented a test yet as I have to finalise the scientific approach to my source and vorticity field representation and interpretation project, I have two ideas that may be appropriate for a range of eventual implementations. First, with anything involving sources/sinks and vortices Green's theorem is a good check in the sense of calculating the circulation along and flux through a curve that wraps around the point. The tolerance for this check would be of a magnitude larger than the magnitude of numerical noise. Another broader check would be to calculate the residual (difference) of the simplified source and vorticity field representation against the ground truth CFD solution. Perfect agreement between the two are not expected due to the fact that the source and vorticity field representation is inherently a simplification of the CFD solution, but a tolerance several order of magnitude smaller than the normalised state variables would likely be a useful starting point.

1 October 2026

Reconstructing a nonlinear compressor stall figure.

This assignment was to reproduce a figure from a scientific paper. As I've been examining compressor rotating stall for the past two years, I decided to look into Moore and Greitzer's 1986 paper on post-stall compressor operating behaviour. The paper's equations outline analytical Galerkin equations, as well as equations for the nonlinear travelling wave problem, which were both solved using MATLAB (written by GPT 5.6 Sol) and plotted over the original data (digitised by GPT 5.6 Sol). The most interesting learning is that one of the four curves involves an adjustment to the nonlinear equations that was mentioned but not fully specified in the paper, which meant that the reproduced results did not perfectly overlap with the original plot.

Developing a scientific plotting skill.

The class emphasises the importance of having a skills markdown file when using AI agents. I established a SKILL.md file containing my requirements and preferences for generating scientific plots. In doing this, I realised the importance of having robust human oversight over the writing of a skills files, rather than giving autonomy to AI agents.
